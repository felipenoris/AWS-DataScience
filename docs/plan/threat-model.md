# Data-leakage threat model

The record [Stage 11](stages/stage-11-dlp.md) step 3 builds: every way data in this estate can be reached
from outside or moved out of it, the control on each, and, where none exists, the acceptance or the open
question that stands in its place. A record is not a control (Lesson 5): an entry here closes nothing, and
an entry marked accepted names who accepted what, when, and what reopens it.

**Each ledger entry has a stable ID, `TM-nn`.** Reference an entry by its ID, never by its position. A
closed entry keeps its ID, struck through, as an `EXC-nn` does in [`AWS_STATE.md`](../AWS_STATE.md).

**This file is pass 0's skeleton, written 2026-10-17 from the repository's readings and the user's Audit
snapshot of that evening (2.1.1).** Step 3's items and 2.1.2's resource types are drafted; the
reachability table's inside column waits on pass 3's internal analyzer; the residuals steps 4, 5 and 6
produce are named and wait on their passes. Pass 4 finishes the file, and the user confirms each
acceptance in it.

## How an entry reads

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **controlled** | a preventive control exists; the entry says whether its effect was measured or only read from code |
| **accepted** | no control; a decision accepted the residual, and the entry names the decision, its date and its revision trigger |
| **open** | no control and no decision; the entry names the decision owed |
| **pending** | the step or stage that produces the reading has not run, and nothing stands in for it |

Evidence carries its kind: *measured* (a run, dated), *read* (code or configuration, dated) or *inferred*
(from documentation, or from code nobody has exercised). An acceptance that only a stage's text states is
marked **the plan's** until the user confirms it at pass 4; an acceptance the user took names the user.

## What the model covers

The accounts that hold governed or derived data today: `Data Governance`, with the lake and the catalog,
and `Sandbox`, with the derived zone, the sandbox lake, the sandbox warehouse and every interactive
surface. `Staging` and `Production` enter when [Stage 9](stages/stage-09-deployment-targets.md) decides
where their results land, and a SageMaker project in either enters only if
[Stage 18](stages/stage-18-staging-deployment-project.md)'s verdict reopens D41 item 2; until then their
entries are pending. The perimeter's three axes are [`architecture.md`](architecture.md) §4.2's and the
egress topology is [D38](decisions/D38-single-egress-hub.md)'s: this file reasons over both and restates
neither.
